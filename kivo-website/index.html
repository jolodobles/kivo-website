Automation earns its place.

Every automation starts with your approval at each step. It runs on its own only after it has proven itself in your business.

Stage 1

Drafts for approval

It prepares the work, such as a reminder email or a job entry. Nothing is sent until you approve it.

Stage 2

Sorts and routes

Once its drafts are reliably right, it files and routes work. Anything unusual still comes to you.

Stage 3

Runs on its own

Routine tasks that have run cleanly for a few weeks run without sign-off. Every action is logged.

Prices, discounts, refunds and commitments to customers always need your sign-off.
