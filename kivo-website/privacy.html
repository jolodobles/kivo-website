Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

Kivo Solutions helps small businesses automate repetitive admin. Questions about your privacy? Contact us at info@kivosolutions.com.au.

What we collect

When you fill in one of our forms (including our website form and Facebook and Instagram lead forms), we collect:

  • your name, phone number and email address
  • information about your business, such as team size and the admin tasks that take up your time
  • any details you share with us on calls or by email
  • recordings of calls, but only if you agree at the start of the call
  • if you take part in a pilot, information in the business tools you give us access to (for example invoices, quotes or job records), used only to build and run your automations

How we use it

We use your information to:

  • contact you about your enquiry and your free admin audit
  • understand your business so we can recommend and set up automations
  • send you follow-up messages about our services. You can opt out at any time.

We don't sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers to run our business, including:

  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram), where some of our forms are hosted
  • HubSpot, which stores our customer records
  • email, SMS and automation tools we use to contact you

Some of these providers may store data outside Australia. We only use reputable providers with appropriate security.

Marketing messages

You can unsubscribe from our emails or reply "STOP" to our texts at any time.

Keeping your information safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access, and we delete it when we no longer need it. We never ask for your passwords: you give us user access to your tools, and you can remove it at any time.

Access and correction

You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by contacting info@kivosolutions.com.au.

Complaints

If you have a concern about your privacy, contact us first at info@kivosolutions.com.au and we'll respond within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
